# Privacy Policy – SalahLK TV

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

SalahLK TV ("the app") is a prayer-time display for mosque and home TVs. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## Summary

**SalahLK TV does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Information the app handles

- **Settings and preferences** (chosen zone, display design, iqamah offsets, and similar) are stored only on your device, using Android's local storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Timetable files.** If you choose to import a prayer timetable from a file on your device, the file is read locally and is not uploaded anywhere.
- **Timetable web address (optional).** If you enter a web address (URL) to download a timetable, the app requests that address directly from the server you specified. That server may see your device's IP address, as with any web request. We do not operate that server and do not receive any of this information.

## Permissions

- **Internet access** (`INTERNET`): used only if you enter a timetable web address, to download that timetable.
- **Run at startup** (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`): lets the app open automatically when the TV turns on.
- **Keep screen awake** (`WAKE_LOCK`): keeps the display on so the clock stays visible.
- **Display over other apps** (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`): lets the app open itself at startup on Android 10 and later.

These permissions are used only for the functions described above.

## Third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not share data with third parties.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
